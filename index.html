Should we wait to book?

WestJet: Currently near floor pricing. Typical range $480–680 CAD. Won't drop further; risk is losing 12 seats together. Book by mid-July.

03 · The Itinerary

Day-by-day, hour-by-hour.

Each meal and night has 2–3 options so the group can vote on the day. Everything is Uber-friendly.

DAY 01

Land & attack

Thurs · Oct 15
Lunch · on arrival

Tacos El Gordo

Downtown · 511 F St

Tijuana-style, multiple meat stations, chaotic and perfect. The adobada (spicy pork) is the move. Order 4–5 each. ~$7 CAD/taco

Afternoon · pick one
A

Balboa Park stroll

Free · 15 min Uber from Gaslamp

Walk it off, hit a craft brewery on the way back, nap before the big night.

B

Early Gaslamp crawl

Gaslamp Quarter

Start the bar crawl at 4 PM — patios are open, places aren't packed yet, easy to grab a section for 12.

Dinner

La Puerta

Gaslamp · 560 Fourth Ave · open until 1 AM

Mexican bar-restaurant. 4.5 stars, 5,000+ reviews. Bold cocktails, packed patio, happy hour deals. Doubles as your first bar of the night. ~$25 CAD pp

Night · pick one
A

Side Bar + Oxford

Gaslamp · upscale clubbing

Boutique nightclub, stylish crowd, DJs spin house/hip-hop/top-40. Bottle service worth it for 12. Then late-round at Oxford Social Club under the Pendry.

$120–180 CAD pp
B

Parq Nightclub

Gaslamp · mega-club energy

Big-name DJs, massive LED displays, booming sound. Best for hard-going group nights. Get a table for 12.

$100–160 CAD pp
C

Pacers Showgirls

Midway · 3334 Midway Dr · ~$20 Uber

SD's #1 rated gentlemen's club. Full kitchen, champagne room. Free entry before 7 PM. Call ahead for a section.

$150–250 CAD pp
DAY 02

Beach day

Fri · Oct 16
Breakfast · pick one
A

Kono's Cafe

PB · foot of Crystal Pier

Line wraps the boardwalk but moves fast. Breakfast burritos = local legend.

~$18 CAD pp
B

Broken Yolk Cafe

PB · Mission Blvd · since 1979

~$28 CAD pp w/ drinks

Madi PB

PB · voted Best Brunch SD

Coconut pancakes, the G.O.A.T. breakfast sandwich, creative mimosa flavours. Reservations required.

~$32 CAD pp
Day · beach

South Mission Beach + Boardwalk

Free · volleyball courts · 3-mile boardwalk

Volleyball courts, bonfires allowed, room for 12. Rent bikes (~$25 CAD/hr) and cruise the boardwalk. Hit Belmont Park's Giant Dipper roller coaster (~$15 CAD) if you're feeling it. Cooler, sunscreen, towels — that's it.

Lahaina Beach House

PB beachfront · opens 9 AM

Beach bar with the best sunset patio in PB. Drink there, return after dinner. ~$14 CAD/beer

Lunch

The Taco Stand

B St (downtown) or PB

Carne asada fries are legendary. Al pastor taco is a must. Quick service, good for a group on the move. ~$20 CAD pp

Night · PB bar crawl

Lahaina → PB Shore Club → 710 Beach Club

All walkable · no Uber needed between

Sunset drinks at Lahaina (5 PM) → walk to PB Shore Club (beachfront, big outdoor screens, open till 2) → end at 710 Beach Club (live music, karaoke, sports bar). PB skews younger and more casual than Gaslamp — easier to hang out at the bar, easier to meet people. $100–150 CAD pp
